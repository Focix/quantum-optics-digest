  1. 76TExtensibly Causally Separable Processes Admit Realizations as Quantum Circuits with Classical Control of Causal Order
    Wenjie Wei, Shengshi Pang · 2026-09-16👍 👎

    A generalised teleportation construction realises every extensibly causally separable process as a circuit with classical control of order, settling an open problem.

    Explain
    In plain words
    Processes where the order of operations is decided on the fly, by earlier measurement results, were suspected to cover a certain well-defined class exactly. This proves they do.
    How it works
    Extensible causal separability asks that a process stay decomposable into 'this party acted first' pieces even when arbitrary ancillas are attached. The proof builds, for any such process, an explicit circuit that teleports states between the parties and uses previous outcomes to pick who acts next.
    Why it matters
    It draws the line cleanly: anything genuinely order-indefinite, the kind of process people hope buys a computational advantage, now cannot be explained away as a classically controlled circuit, and the converse direction is no longer an open question.
    Be skeptical of
    The construction is a realisation in principle; the teleportation overhead is not costed, so this says what is possible, not what an experiment would build.
  2. 74TOn the applicability of Kolmogorov's theory of probability to the description of quantum phenomena. Part II: Bell inequalities
    Maik Reddiger · 2026-09-25👍 👎

    Letting detector settings shift the distribution of hidden detector parameters reproduces CHSH violation inside Kolmogorov probability, so locality need not be what fails.

    Explain
    In plain words
    A careful argument that violating Bell's inequality does not force you to give up ordinary probability theory or locality, if the detectors themselves are allowed to behave randomly in a setting-dependent way.
    How it works
    In the contextual Bell models studied here, each detector carries its own random parameter whose distribution depends on the setting chosen for that detector. Within Kolmogorov's framework this suffices to reproduce the CHSH violation, and the paper shows that even ordinary classical Bell-type experiments motivate such a description.
    Why it matters
    It relocates what a Bell violation rules out. If the loophole is this cheap, the interesting claim is not 'nature is nonlocal' but 'detector behaviour cannot be treated as setting-independent', which is a statement about apparatus, not about spacelike separation.
    Be skeptical of
    Setting-dependent detector parameters are close to the measurement-dependence loophole, and the paper argues the notion is physically natural rather than demonstrating any experiment where it is needed.
  3. 72TCatalytic Activation of Genuine Multipartite Entanglement and Nonlocality
    Eliot Donnadieu, Pavel Sekatski, Nicolas Brunner et al. · 2026-09-16👍 👎

    A catalyst plus local operations turns any biseparable state that is not partition-separable into a genuinely multipartite entangled and nonlocal one, with no classical communication.

    Explain
    In plain words
    Some states look like mixtures of two-party entanglement, with no genuine three-way entanglement. Borrowing a helper state, which you get back unchanged, converts them into genuinely multipartite entangled states.
    How it works
    The 'sum-to-product' protocol turns a mixture of states into their tensor product in a heralded way; applied to network states, which are biseparable by construction because each link is only bipartite, it produces genuine multipartite entanglement and genuine multipartite Bell nonlocality using local operations alone.
    Why it matters
    Genuine multipartite entanglement was treated as a property a state either has or lacks. Catalysis shows the classification is not closed under free operations, which changes what a quantum network built from pairwise links can in principle certify.
    Be skeptical of
    Deterministic on the target but heralded in the protocol, and it needs states that are biseparable yet not partition-separable, so it does not manufacture genuine multipartite entanglement from nothing.
  4. 70TNear-Optimal Bell Nonlocality with GKP and Entangled Cat States
    Özlem Erkılıç, Aritra Das, S. Nibedita Swain et al. · 2026-09-23👍 👎

    Photon-number-resolving detection with Gaussian preprocessing and feed-forward nearly saturates CHSH violation for finite-energy GKP Bell states, without non-Clifford gates.

    Explain
    In plain words
    Grid-encoded states of light can show Bell nonlocality even when they are only weakly squeezed, but the obvious measurements miss it; counting photons instead finds nearly all of it.
    How it works
    Logical Pauli measurements on finite-energy GKP Bell states reveal no violation, and reaching the state's maximum CHSH value normally needs logical non-Clifford rotations. The authors move the non-Gaussian resource into the measurement: photon-number-resolving detection with Gaussian preprocessing and adaptive feed-forward approaches the maximum violation.
    Why it matters
    GKP states in circuits are far from fault-tolerance-grade squeezing, so the useful question is what sub-threshold GKP states are good for. It also makes the general point that a hard non-Gaussian operation can sometimes be moved from the state preparation into the detector.
    Be skeptical of
    Optical photon-number-resolving detection at the required efficiency is itself hard, and the analysis is a numerical study under chosen measurement constraints rather than a proposal with a full loss budget.
  5. 66TThe Generalised Causality Principle
    Sahil Gopalkrishna Naik · 2026-09-19👍 👎

    Proposes a causality principle bounding which process-matrix correlations are physical when each party acts once, a constraint the formalism has lacked.

    Explain
    In plain words
    The framework used to describe processes without a fixed order of events allows correlations that may be unphysical, and nobody knew what principle should rule them out. This proposes one.
    How it works
    Just as no-signalling constrains Bell correlations without assuming quantum theory, the generalised causality principle constrains the correlations a process matrix can produce under the single-interaction constraint, where each party touches the environment only once.
    Why it matters
    Indefinite causal order is normally justified by analogy with nonlocality, and the analogy was missing its no-signalling. Having a candidate principle is what makes device-independent statements about causal order possible.
    Be skeptical of
    A proposed principle, not a derivation from anything more primitive, and its physical justification is as contestable as no-signalling once was.
  6. 64TProcrastinating einselection in non-Markovian quantum dynamics
    Michael J. Moody, Tara Kalsi, Agung Budiyono et al. · 2026-09-17👍 👎

    A speed limit for observables shows non-Markovian backflow delays pointer-state emergence, so einselection can be postponed rather than only slowed.

    Explain
    In plain words
    Environments normally pick out a preferred set of classical-looking states quickly. When the environment feeds information back, that selection can be held off.
    How it works
    The authors derive a quantum speed limit for how fast an observable can move towards its pointer value under time-local open dynamics, then identify two ways non-Markovianity raises that speed: dissipative motion supported by population revival, and restored asymmetry enabling coherent motion. Accumulated negativity in the dynamics is what delays permanent einselection.
    Why it matters
    Superconducting qubits sit in structured environments, and the emergence of a preferred basis is usually assumed to be instantaneous. A bound that ties the delay to measurable non-Markovianity turns a conceptual story into something with a timescale.
    Be skeptical of
    The bound applies to differentiable time-local dynamics in finite dimensions, which excludes strongly structured baths, and delay is quantified for chosen models rather than measured.
  7. 62TThe Uncertainty Principle, Uncertainty Relations, and Underlying Trajectories: Feynman, Nelson, Bohm, and Persistent Kac-Dirac Dynamics
    Partha Ghose · 2026-09-24👍 👎

    Separates the statistical uncertainty relations from the ontological ban on trajectories, with finite-speed Kac dynamics mapping onto the Dirac equation.

    Explain
    In plain words
    Two different claims travel under the name 'uncertainty principle': a statement about the spread of measurement results, and a stronger claim that particles have no trajectories at all. Only the first follows from quantum mechanics.
    How it works
    Feynman paths, Nelson's stochastic mechanics, Bohmian mechanics and Kac's finite-speed persistent random walk are compared. Feynman paths show Brownian short-time scaling, Nelson derives Schrodinger dynamics from diffusion plus extra assumptions, Bohmian mechanics keeps trajectories and the predictions, and Wick-rotated Kac dynamics maps to the Dirac equation.
    Why it matters
    It is a clean piece of hygiene about what the experiments actually exclude, and the Kac-to-Dirac mapping is the concrete part: a finite-speed trajectory model reproducing relativistic quantum kinematics is not what the folklore expects.
    Be skeptical of
    Mostly a synthesis of known positions, and the Kac mapping is presented as a formal correspondence rather than a physical model with independent predictions.
  8. 61TForbidden Subspaces in Quantum State Smoothing
    Chon-Fai Kam, Kai-Wen Wong · 2026-09-15👍 👎

    Positivity fixes when a pre- and post-selected state is orderable; otherwise it certifies contextuality, and the forbidden subspace's parity labels the record's topological class.

    Explain
    In plain words
    What state should you assign to a system between preparation and a later successful post-selection? Demanding only that the answer be a legitimate positive state already decides the question in some cases and forces contextuality in others.
    How it works
    The post-selection forbids a subspace. If that subspace is spanned by eigenvectors of a full-rank filtered state, an interval of operator orderings around the symmetric one stays positive; if not, no ordering works and contextuality is certified, on a qubit. The averaged filtered state carries the record's entanglement spectrum, so symmetry makes the smallest allowed forbidden subspaces even-dimensional in the Haldane phase and odd in the trivial one.
    Why it matters
    Smoothed states are what you write down when you continuously monitor a qubit and then condition on a later outcome, which is exactly circuit-QED practice, and the paper says when that assignment is forced and when it is a choice.
    Be skeptical of
    Extremely compressed, and the topological reading rests on identifying the record's entanglement spectrum with a symmetry-protected class, which is a strong structural assumption.
  9. 60TCorrelation and Branching Mechanisms in the Quantum-to-Classical Transition of Interacting Systems
    Bingyu Cui · 2026-09-14👍 👎

    Bohmian flow diagnoses when a retained coordinate closes into classical dynamics, and why Ehrenfest fails once spin-position covariances build up.

    Explain
    In plain words
    Three simple models show what has to be true for one part of an interacting system to follow its own classical equation, and what breaks when it does not.
    How it works
    Bohmian trajectories are used only as a readout of the full wavefunction's probability flow. A closed classical description emerges either when interaction-induced correlations are suppressed or when the observable does not feel the remaining ones. A hard-core collision generates nonseparability through tail reflection; in the Rabi model spin-position covariance kills the mean-field Ehrenfest approximation; in a state-dependent force the total mean position still obeys an exact law.
    Why it matters
    Mean-field or Ehrenfest treatments are the default when a qubit is coupled to a mechanical or classical-looking degree of freedom, and this gives the covariance condition under which that default is safe.
    Be skeptical of
    Three toy models chosen to isolate mechanisms; there is no general criterion, and the Bohmian machinery is a diagnostic here rather than a claim about ontology.
  10. 58TOn the Born rule in a new quantum approach
    Inge S. Helland · 2026-09-21👍 👎

    Derives the Born rule from a transition-probability postulate akin to the likelihood principle of statistics, with a leaner notion of state.

    Explain
    In plain words
    Another attempt to show that the squared-amplitude rule for quantum probabilities is not an extra assumption but follows from something weaker and more intuitive.
    How it works
    The derivation proceeds in stages from what the author calls the transition probability principle, a condition closely related to the likelihood principle in statistics, and ends with a simpler state concept for finite-dimensional quantum mechanics.
    Why it matters
    How much of quantum mechanics is forced and how much is stipulated is the central question of every reconstruction programme, and the Born rule is the most contested stipulation.
    Be skeptical of
    One instalment of the author's ongoing programme; whether the transition probability principle is genuinely weaker than what it derives, rather than the Born rule in other clothes, is exactly what a reader has to judge.
