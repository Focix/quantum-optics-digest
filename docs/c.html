  1. 84TPlenitude of ideal correlations explains quantum theory
    Adán Cabello · 2026-11-16👍 👎

    Cabello derives Hilbert space and the Born rule from one principle: every correlation allowed when all observables have ideal measurements is realized.

    Explain
    In plain words
    They show that quantum theory's mathematical structure follows from one rule about which correlations nature allows.
    How it works
    Assume every observable can be measured ideally (repeatable, not disturbing compatible ones); then demand that every correlation such a theory permits actually occurs. Only Hilbert space with the Born rule fits.
    Why it matters
    It is a compact answer to 'why this theory', built on measurement properties that experimentalists can test, including with qubits.
    Be skeptical of
    The 'plenitude' principle is strong and its physical motivation is debatable; the abstract gives the claim, not how loopholes are handled.

    Explain
    In plain words
