    Anatoly Kulikov, Josua D. Schär, Alonso Hernández-Antón et al. · 2026-10-04👍 👎

    Two qubits in separate cryostats 30 m apart get a tunable-phase CZ gate from one reflected microwave photon; remote readout needs no remote-side action.

    Explain
    In plain words
    They linked two superconducting qubits in different fridges with a cold cable and made them interact by bouncing one microwave photon off the far end.
    How it works
    A photon emitted at node A reflects off a cavity at node B; B's qubit state sets the phase the photon picks up, and changing the photon frequency dials that phase from 0 to π.
    Why it matters
    It is a passive, synchronisation-free way to entangle remote superconducting nodes, the basic step of a microwave quantum network beyond one cryostat.

    Explain
    In plain words
    They watched a tiny vibrating beam jump between zero and one quantum of vibration, one jump at a time.
    How it works
