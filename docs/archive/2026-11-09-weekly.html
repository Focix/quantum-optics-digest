    Toyofumi Ishikawa, Shumpei Masuda, Akiyoshi Tomonaga et al. · 2026-11-05👍 👎

    Reflection spectra of a Kerr parametric oscillator near a degeneracy need dissipative coupling between transition coherences; independent-line models fail.

    Explain
    In plain words
    They shone microwaves on a driven superconducting artificial atom and saw spectral lines that only make sense if two transitions interfere through their shared loss.
    How it works
    Strong parametric driving pushes two transitions of a Kerr parametric oscillator almost on top of each other; the reflected microwave lineshape is then fit with and without a term that couples the two decaying coherences, and only the coupled model works.
    Why it matters
    It is a new interference channel in open-system spectroscopy of artificial atoms, so lineshapes you might blame on extra modes or noise could instead be this effect.
    Be skeptical of
    The evidence is a better model fit to reflection spectra at one operating point, not an independent signature such as photon statistics.
