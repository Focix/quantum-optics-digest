    Seunghan Lee, Woochan Jung, Jayeon Kim et al. · 2026-10-24👍 👎

    Calibrated graphene Josephson calorimeter resolves 2.9 zJ pulses and recovers in 20 ns; pins the gap to ideal on heat capacity and phonon coupling.

    Explain
    In plain words
    They built a tiny thermometer out of graphene that can feel a few zeptojoules of microwave energy and reset in tens of nanoseconds, and worked out why it is not yet as sensitive as theory promises.
    How it works
    Absorbed microwave energy heats graphene's electrons, which switches a Josephson junction; calibrated nanosecond pulses and two-pulse thermometry measure the heat capacity and cooling rate of the same device.
    Why it matters
    Graphene bolometers are a leading route to counting single itinerant microwave photons; knowing that heat capacity is 12x and phonon coupling 50x too large tells you exactly what to fix.
    Be skeptical of

    Explain
    In plain words

    How it works

    Explain

    How it works
