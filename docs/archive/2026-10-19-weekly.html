    Yan Li, Yunfan Yang, Zenghui Bao et al. · 2026-10-14👍 👎

    Bell-state projection of flying microwave photons fuses on-demand time-bin cluster states deterministically, reaching genuine 13-photon entanglement with emitter reset and reuse.

    Explain
    In plain words
    They stitched small batches of entangled microwave photons into one bigger entangled web, and the stitching works every time rather than by luck.
    How it works
    A superconducting emitter makes short chains of entangled photons in time bins; a second circuit catches pairs of photons and checks, without destroying them, which Bell state they share, which glues the chains together. The emitter qubit is reset and reused between chains.
    Why it matters
    Fusing is the step that turns a single emitter into a source of arbitrarily large photonic resource states, and doing it deterministically with microwave circuits removes the usual probabilistic overhead.

    Explain
    In plain words
    Two qubits far apart on the same microwave line normally talk to each other at a fixed strength; here they shake the qubit frequencies to dial that strength up and down at will.
    How it works

    How it works
