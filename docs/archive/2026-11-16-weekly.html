    Explain
    In plain words
    An atom in a long cavity keeps re-absorbing its own echo, and the emitted light briefly develops a comb of evenly spaced colours.
    How it works
    Light leaves the atom, bounces off the far mirror and returns after a fixed round trip; an exact delay-equation solution tracks how each emission stage interferes with the previous one in the time-resolved spectrum.
