# Weekly — superconducting quantum computing, what is hot

From the week's computing-tagged papers pick the ten a researcher in superconducting qubits would want to have heard of:

- New records: coherence, gate fidelity, readout, qubit count with a real benchmark.
- New qubit designs, couplers, or architectures with experimental evidence.
- Error-correction milestones: logical qubits, below-threshold results, real-time decoding.
- Results from major groups (Google, IBM, Quantinuum-scale efforts, Yale, ETH, Delft, Chalmers, USTC, RIKEN, AWS, Rigetti, IQM, Alibaba, Zuchongzhi).
- Anything already collecting citations (see the Semantic Scholar count when present).

Prefer results over surveys, experiments over proposals, and papers that change what people will try next.
