# C. Dark matter with superconducting devices

Proposals and results using superconducting qubits, superconducting resonators, single microwave photon detectors, or quantum sensing protocols (squeezing, photon counting, entanglement-assisted sensing) to search for axions, dark photons, hidden photons or other dark matter candidates.

Include instrumentation papers if the detector is qubit-based or photon-counting based, and haloscope papers that use quantum-limited or sub-quantum-limited readout.

Exclude pure astrophysics, cosmology without a laboratory detector, and detectors with no quantum-device component (classical amplifier haloscopes only get a low score).
