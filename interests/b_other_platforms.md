# B. Quantum optics on other platforms — ranked for novelty

Platforms: neutral atoms, Rydberg atoms, trapped ions, quantum dots, colour centres, molecules, optomechanics, photonic integrated circuits, atomic ensembles.

Already standard, score low unless there is a genuine twist: transmission and reflection measurements of a single emitter coupled to an open transmission line or waveguide, including extinction, Mollow triplet, power broadening, photon blockade in a single cavity, standard single-photon source characterisation (purity, indistinguishability numbers), yet another squeezing record without a new mechanism.

Score high: experiments or theory showing an effect, regime or protocol not obvious from the standard cavity/waveguide QED toolbox. Examples: new bound states or non-Markovian effects, chiral or giant-atom physics with a new consequence, collective effects with a measurable signature, photon-photon interactions in a new regime, protocols that transfer to superconducting circuits, unexpected correlations or statistics, measurement-induced effects on light.

The reader works with superconducting circuits. Prefer results that could be reproduced or exploited on that platform.
